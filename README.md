# CTF Writeups — Shreya Bhat

This repository is where I share my PicoCTF and other CTF writeups — showing **how I solved** challenges and what I learned along the way.

---

## What you’ll find
In each writeup:  
- I explain the problem in a simple way.  
- I show the steps I used to solve it.  
- I write down the problems I faced and notes about the process.  
- I might include scripts or screenshots sometimes, but not always.

---

## 🚀 Repo goals
- Share my learning and problem‑solving process.  
- Get suggestions and corrections from others if I made mistakes.  
- Keep things safe and respectful (no full flags or sensitive data).
